INDONESIA

is a company that focus on development and application of information technology, especially on Intelligence Information Technology & Intelligence Equipment, Counter Surveillance (TSCM), and tactical airspace defense systems. Since our establishment, we have carried out extensive research and technological developments in collaboration with institutional partners, creating specialized solutions tailored to Indonesian strategic conditions.
